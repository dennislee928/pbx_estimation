Key Findings

  • 156 solutions across countries/regions — Asia-Pacific has 45 entries, Europe has 47, and the Americas have 48.
  • 21 solutions classified EOL — Legacy PBX remains a migration and security risk in installed bases.
  • 101 solutions classified Cutting Edge — Reflects the rapid pace of cloud/API/AI communication platform innovation globally.
  • Taiwan shows a clear bifurcation: EVOX/CHT cloud PBX for new deployments vs huge legacy installed base of Panasonic/Toshiba EOL equipment.
  • The crawler-enriched catalog now includes CPaaS, open-source PBX, regional telco hosted PBX, and IoT/eSIM connectivity options for edge-device command paths.
